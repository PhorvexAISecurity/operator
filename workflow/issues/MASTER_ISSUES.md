### M-144 · λ decays to its floor and is structurally trapped there — after a brief seed-decay, the cost stack becomes inert · **OPEN** · `ECONOMICS`

*Evidence history (claims retain their original versions):*

**What:** `internal/cost/dual.go`: λ seeds at 0.6 (`DefaultLambdaSeed`), step 0.05, clamp [0,10]. `Step()` clamps `excess = demand−budget` to −1 whenever demand < budget−1, so a fleet selecting ~1 move/tick against `maxMovesPerTick: 3` decays λ by 0.0167/tick → 0.6 reaches 0 in ~36 ticks (≈6–9 min at campaign cadence). The only path upward requires `demand > budget` **and** `spent ≥ budget` (the P-REDTEAM λ-starvation fix gating rises on the budget being genuinely binding) — a fleet that never consumes its full budget can never raise λ again. The anti-decoy fix for the ceiling-pin manufactured the floor-trap: same defect class as C1/M-98 worried about, **opposite sign** — production pins at the floor and vetoes nothing.
**Consequence:** after λ reaches its floor, the objective's `−λ·C` term is identically zero. In the captured decision-record window, selected records' attribution arrays read `cost:-lambda*C contribution: 0`, so **net = coverageValue exactly**. The entire cost stack (RLS, conformal bound, `action_flow`, the SLO ledger) is then computed and logged with no effect on selection. This makes M-139 worse than filed: the per-dollar cost thesis is not merely measured for 1 of 15 generatable mutating actions — after the brief seed-decay the cost channel is **unused**, and "cheapest effective actuator" is not being evaluated in steady state. (H7-cost measurement remains valid and becomes more valuable: cost figures are still computed and logged, and the day λ un-pins, their correctness matters immediately.)
**Live evidence (Round 1, 2026-08-25, `phorvex:0.4.50`):** λ = 0.0000 for all six tenants across 15,239 deduplicated audit records; 920/920 vllm selections with perDollar 22.9–29.2 against λ=0 (any positive per-dollar passes the gate). **Observed under ambient load:** the cluster-wide loadgen (all six production services, diurnal) ran for the entire window — so the claim is not "an idle fleet never contends for the budget" but "**even a loaded fleet never contends**": `demand` counts assets with positive-coverage moves, not traffic volume.
**Live evidence, refined (analysed 2026-08-30 from the pinned Round-1 corpus, `phorvex:0.4.50` / `b163c69`; `metrics-brain.txt`):** 355 non-empty metric scrapes over a **separate capture window — 2026-08-25T14:32:36Z → 17:32:57Z (3h00m)**, which overlaps the 4h02m audit window by only 1h47m — contain **2,041 tenant-scrape samples**: 355 each for `cv1-control`, `litellm`, `nats`, `vllm`, `voice` and 266 for `cv1-stress`, which was created mid-capture (so 2,041, not 355 × 6 = 2,130). λ is strictly positive on **12 of 2,041 samples (0.59%)** — of which one is the 6.94e-17 float residue of zero, so **11 carry a meaningful value (0.54%)** — and the observed nonzero samples form two monotone decay ladders off the seed, with no sampled rise:

| tenant | scrape # | λ sequence |
|---|---|---|
| `cv1-control` | 1 → 7 | 0.50 · 0.40 · 0.30 · 0.25 · 0.15 · 0.10 · 6.94e-17 |
| `cv1-stress` | 90 → 94 | 0.45 · 0.35 · 0.25 · 0.15 · 0.05 |

`cv1-stress` first appears at scrape 90 because the tenant was created mid-campaign — so both ladders are the same *kind* of event: a policy starting at `DefaultLambdaSeed = 0.6` (`internal/cost/dual.go:26`) and decaying to the floor in **5–7 observed scrapes (≈2.5–3.5 min)**, after which each remains 0 for the rest of the metrics capture. Across all tenants, 2,029 of 2,041 tenant-scrape samples (99.41%) are exactly 0.0, and a further one is the float residue. The four tenants whose policies predate the capture are 0 on **all 355 scrapes** — they had already finished decaying before recording began.

This upgrades the finding from a code derivation to a **sampled trajectory**: the decay mechanism derived from `Step()` is observed on two tenants, and every observed nonzero transition descends. No scrape observed a rise. The 30-second cadence cannot exclude an inter-scrape rise followed by decay, and `demand` is not exported, so the stronger claim that demand “never once raised λ” is not supported by this corpus. The terminal `6.94e-17` is consistent with the source's repeated subtraction and floating-point residue.

The audit-side claim is unaffected and independently confirmed: λ appears as `0.00` in the `reason` string of **15,239 of 15,239 unique decision records** (and is absent from the structured field on all of them — see M-146).

**Config gate:** budget contention. Un-pins only on a fleet with demand ≥ budget sustained — and the obvious test lever is blocked by M-22 (`maxMovesPerTick: 0` returns 3, so the smallest expressible budget is 1).
**Fix:** give λ a floor above 0 when the policy intends cost to matter (or an explicit `lambdaLocked` semantics); reconsider gating rises on `spent >= budget` (demand exclusion already signals binding); note the coupling with M-22 — the budget knob cannot express 0, which is the natural way to force contention in a test.
**The instructive part (Claude, 020):** the trap is manufactured by this file's own documented P-REDTEAM fix. The `spent >= budget` gate was added to stop a decoy-pumped λ rocket — an adversarial-hardening change. It works, and it also makes the price unreachable from below on any under-contended fleet: **a defensive patch that closed an attack and disabled the mechanism it was protecting.** This is exactly the class of change the fix loop's Class-B band (predicted-output range on decision records) would have caught, had it existed — the canonical example for why that band matters.

### M-143 · pHold is algebraically pinned to 1 by pod age — the Bayesian belief layer is annihilated, not outweighed · **OPEN** · `CLOCK->BELIEF`

Severity: MAJOR · Tier: 1 · Fixes: none

> Anatomy join review: **required**.
> `V2` **LOCUS** · belief
> matched on `belief`
> `V2` TOUCHES · killchain · runner.policies · runner.feedback

*Evidence history (claims retain their original versions):*

**What:** `internal/killchain/hazard.go:324-347`: `exposurePressure = clamp01(exposureSec/proactiveFloorSec)`; once a pod's age passes the floor, `exposurePressure = 1` and the noisy-OR collapses — `pHoldRaw = betaMean + 1·(1−betaMean) = 1`, identically, for every value of betaMean — and the just-moved damping → 1 on the same clock. For any pod older than its policy's `proactiveFloor` (15m in all six deployed policies), **"probability the attacker holds" is a restatement of "this pod has not been rotated recently."** The per-asset alpha/beta memory, the tenant prior, and the symmetric memory feedback have zero effect past the floor. Broader than M-25/M-26: those need GPU + co-residency + the classifier; this needs only pod age.
**Live evidence (Round 1, 2026-08-25, `phorvex:0.4.50`):** pHold ≥ 0.999 in 12,898 of 15,239 deduplicated audit records (84.6%), and in 100.0% of the four pre-existing tenants; a deliberately bounced pod (`cv1-control`) showed the exact predicted signature — pHold ramping 0.07 → 0.99 over precisely 15 minutes, then pinned at 1.00.
**Config gate:** `ProactiveFloor` — but the knob is **structurally unmovable**: it is simultaneously the proactive-rotation cadence, the pHold saturation clock, and (per M-29, two sites) the event-age decay horizon. No setting of it lets pHold carry information while proactive rotation still works. File as structurally gated, not mis-tuned.
**Scope refinement (Claude, 018 — his own narrower hypothesis tested and rejected):** with `defaultAntiOverRotation = 5m` and the floor at 15m, pHold is **informative and actionable for ~10 minutes after each rotation** (5–15m window), then dead for the remainder of the pod's life. On a fleet whose pods live for days that is a vanishing fraction of uptime — and since a rotation resets the clock, the signal is only ever alive in the wake of a move already made. **Config amplifier:** the shipped default floor is 1h; every deployed policy sets 15m, shrinking the informative window ~5× by config choice, not only by structure. Raising the floor toward the default is a cheap *partial* mitigation available today — not free: the triple-loaded knob slows proactive rotation and stretches event decay in step. **Reproduction note:** on a default-configured cluster the pin arrives at 1h, not 15m.
**Fix:** structural — a noisy-OR whose second term saturates cannot carry evidence. Decouple the saturation clock from the cadence knob (dedicated constants), and make the exposure-pressure term a *prior floor*, not an annihilator (e.g. cap its contribution below 1, or multiply rather than noisy-OR).
*Sources: Claude derivation + H17 hypothesis (bus 014), K3 code-verified and live-falsifier-confirmed at `v0.4.50`; scope refinement bus 018.*
