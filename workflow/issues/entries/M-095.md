### M-95 · The adapter-poison fact is attacker-steerable · **HIGH** · 🟠 STATIC · `KNOWLEDGE`
**What:** `SuspectAdapter` is projected verbatim from a pod annotation whenever `lora-enabled=true`; its fact is weighted to dominate selection. Anyone who can annotate the pod can force or suppress adapter-rotate. (The T22 demo steered it by hand — fine for a demo, not a boundary.)
**Fix:** only trusted detector identities may set the marker (same allowlist as M-94).
*Sources: A-114.*
