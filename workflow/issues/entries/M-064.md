### M-64 · Epoch-write conflicts discard completed effects · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION->EFFECT`
**What:** kv-reset flushes the cache (irreversible) and THEN writes the epoch with a plain Update on a stale resourceVersion. A routine conflict returns an error — the flush happened but is recorded as failure, the session clock never resets, and the brain re-selects the flush next tick (a second churn driver alongside M-28). No handler uses RetryOnConflict or Patch.
**Fix:** RetryOnConflict with fresh Get, or merge-Patch the annotation; when the side effect landed, a bookkeeping failure must not erase the credit.
*Sources: A-88.*
