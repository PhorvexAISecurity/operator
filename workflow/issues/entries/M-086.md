### M-86 · The wedge-reconciler family: dry-run bypass, selector-blind, unconditional, error-swallowed, unaudited · **MAJOR** · 🔴 LIVE · `CONTROL`
**What:** `reconcileWedged` runs every tick over every namespace with any policy, gated only on the kill-switch — BEFORE the snapshot that carries the dry-run annotations, and never consulting per-tenant dry-run or the policy selector. `ResumeWarmSwaps` deletes any pod carrying two attacker-writable labels (`swap-group` + `role=retiring`) with no owner check, no drain, no leader-existence check; `pickSurvivor` can delete the only Ready leader against a Deployment-owned survivor (the live F18 loop); it is blind to model-shard/parallel-group swaps; all errors are discarded; none of it is audited or metered.
**Live proof (second pass, 2026-08-21):** in a throwaway namespace with the dry-run brake on (`phorvex.ai/dry-run: "true"`) AND a `mode: dryRun` policy selecting only `app=victim`, a decoy pod carrying just the two labels (`phorvex.ai/swap-group`, `phorvex.ai/role=retiring`) — and NOT matching the policy selector — was **deleted within ~10 seconds**, while still Pending. The brain's own log records it: `warm-swap resume: converged wedged swap {"namespace": "pxverify", "reaped": 1}`. One log line is the entire audit trail for an unaudited delete that bypassed every brake.
**Fix:** move after CollectSnapshot; pass only active, non-dry-run namespaces + the tenant selector; require a Phorvex-managed-by marker; audit every reconciler mutation.
*Sources: A-2, A-5, A-12, A-31, A-90, A-162, A-175, A-202, A-213, A-214, A-218, I-18; live-confirmed test V1 (2026-08-21).*
