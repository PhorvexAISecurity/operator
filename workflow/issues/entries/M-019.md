### M-19 · No failure feedback: a permanently-failing move is re-selected forever · **MAJOR** · 🔴 LIVE (third pass) · `CHOICE`
**What:** On Actuate error the runner logs and `continue`s. No failure counter, no backoff, no circuit-break, no same-tick fallback to the pod's next-best action, and the decision layer is never told. Reachable permanent-failure loop, verified in code: a GPU pod without guardrail labels mints the guardrailBypass fact unconditionally → guardrail-rotate is selected (cheap, dominant cover) → the handler hard-errors (no guardrail tier) → repeat every tick, while rotate/quarantine would have worked.
**Live evidence (V10, 2026-08-21):** the dead-endpoint kv-reset rig exhibited exactly this loop — `actuate failed` at 01:54:32, then the very next tick (01:54:42) selected the same action for the same pod with an identical score line and failed again, at ~10-20s cadence, with no backoff, no cooldown, no fallback, and no change in the decision narrative. A permanently-failing move is re-selected forever, observed.
**Fix:** per-(pod,action) failure records with cooldowns fed into the candidate generator as exclusions; next-best re-selection within the tick.
*Sources: A-25, A-43; V10 live 2026-08-21.*
