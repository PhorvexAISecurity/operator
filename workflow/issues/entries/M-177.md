### M-177 · Strict MIG and adapter profile JSON accepts ambiguous case aliases · **MEDIUM** · NEW · `ACTUATION`

**Evidence:** workflow/assessments/20260916-local-continuation/reconciliation/strict-profile-json/REPORT.md
