### M-33 · `identity-rotate` / `route-rotate` are label-only and coverless · **MAJOR** · 🔴 LIVE (T10/T11) · `KNOWLEDGE`
**What:** Both handlers write one pod label and nothing else — no SA-token invalidation (the token stays valid for its full TTL), no Service/EndpointSlice change (the pinned route survives). Their recon cover grants were deliberately removed (PHVX-9e), so they are **structurally unselectable** (zero marginal gain), yet they remain in the vocabulary, the docs, and two shipped test results (T10/T11) that are not reproducible at HEAD.
**Currency (`v0.4.64`):** R-3 made `route-rotate`'s Service restitch real. The cover/selectability half of this entry still needs a re-screen; the leftover SLO lie (zero-impact justification that was not updated) is **M-147**, not this ID.
**Fix:** either implement the real effect (TokenRequest/SA rotation; Service restitch) or mark them declared-only and annotate the old tests.
