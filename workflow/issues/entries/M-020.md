### M-20 · The capability witness never gates selection · **MINOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `CONTRACT->CHOICE`
**What:** `unsupportedEnabledActions` computes a well-shaped per-action capability check (warm pools, MIG annotations, serving endpoints) but only writes a status condition — the optimizer never sees it. Fleet-wide OR semantics also mark an action "supported" for pods it can't serve.
**Fix:** feed per-action support into the candidate generator.
*Sources: A-203.*
