### M-148 · Three compensation writes discard their error — a failed rollback is silent · **HIGH** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** The undo path exists, and its failure is dropped. Three compensation sites use `_ = ec.Client.Update(...)`:
- `handlers_ai.go:138` — warm-swap and model-shard-replace share `warmCutover`; on flip-down failure the shadow is re-labelled `shadow`. If that write fails **both pods stay `leader`**. The handler returns only the flip-down error; the ledger records `OutcomeFailed` about the action and is silent about the split-brain.
- `handlers_ai_p4set.go:97` — model-shard-replace rolls already-flipped ranks back to shadow; each `_ = Update` can fail mid-group.
- `handlers_ai_p4set.go:276` — request-drain restores the serving label after a failed in-flight abort so the pod is not stranded unready; the restore error is discarded.

Two success-path writes also discard errors (`handlers_ai.go:128` flipping marker; `handlers_ai_p4set.go:112` old-leader retire). The control is guardrail-rotate `:353`, which `errors.Join`s a failed live-variant restore.
**Not M-67.** M-67 is drain abort after flip with *no* rollback. Here a rollback exists and its failure is dropped.
**Fix:** check every compensation write; join or return the restore error; do not leave two leaders labelled when the re-flip fails.
