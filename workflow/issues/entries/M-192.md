### M-192 · Persisted catalogue jobs reject console fsGroup permissions after rollout · **MEDIUM** · NEW · `CONSOLE`

**Evidence:** intake/RPT-20260924-083628-persisted-catalogue-jobs-reject-console-fsgroup/VERIFICATION.md
