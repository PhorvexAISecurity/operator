### M-47 · `rotate` deletes unowned pods permanently — and narrates a respawn · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** No owner check anywhere on the path; the availability floor exempts ownerless pods outright; the handler note says "owner respawns clean" and the console renders "Replaced the compromised instance with a clean one." A bare pod (kubectl-run, Helm hook, static manifest) is deleted forever. Silent, irreversible, and mis-narrated.
**Fix:** refuse when no controller ownerRef exists (fail-closed), plus a feasibility law vetoing rotate on ownerless pods.
*Sources: A-57.*
