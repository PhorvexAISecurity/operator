### M-162 · Credential-theft oracle replaces the captured credential with a new unbound token · **HIGH** · NEW · `VERIFICATION`

**What:** c1-credtheft-rbac discards the stolen prefix and ignores exec failure; its oracle mints and replays a new unbound TokenRequest credential. Failed theft can report success and the original pod-bound token's survival is unmeasurable.

**Evidence:** intake/RPT-20260910-173400-credential-provenance/VERIFICATION.md

**Required correction:** Retain the real per-trial credential only in memory, establish baseline usability, replay that same value after response, distinguish identity binding and authn/authz/transport failure, and never mint a substitute or log token bytes.
