### M-134 · Phoenix adoption race: the first actuations after policy creation are rejected and lost · **HIGH** · 🔴 LIVE · `ACTUATION`
**What:** Phoenix's SecurityEvent reconciler refuses events whose target pods are not yet AMTD-managed (`Pod "victim-1" is a SecurityEvent target but not AMTD managed` — `securityevent_controller.go:124`). Adoption happens asynchronously via the AMTD controller after the brain's `EnsurePolicy` creates the AMTD CR. Live-observed 2026-08-21: the first event fired within seconds of policy creation errored out; only events after adoption completed executed. The brain is never told any of this (M-9), so a policy's opening moves — often the most urgent, right after a detector's first alert — are exactly the ones that can vanish.
**Fix:** the brain should treat "target not AMTD-managed" as a retryable state (re-queue the move, or have EnsurePolicy block until adoption confirms); Phoenix should requeue rejected events instead of dropping them.
*Sources: second-pass live test V2 (2026-08-21).*
