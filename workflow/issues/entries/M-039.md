### M-39 · `kv-migrate-invalidate`: local flush, full session credit · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `KNOWLEDGE`
**What:** The local invalidate is real, but the cross-worker leg (`MigrateRemote`) is hardwired to fail — on a disaggregated P/D deployment the decode peers keep the poisoned KV while the session fact is booked covered and the session epoch stamped (which also locks out further local flushes via the cadence guard).
**Fix:** no session credit (and no epoch) when peers are declared and unconfirmed; per-peer result accounting.
*Sources: A-49, A-110, A-132.*
