### M-51 · Quarantine orphans the pod from garbage collection · **HIGH** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** `pod.OwnerReferences = nil` removes ALL ownerRefs — the pod becomes invisible to cascade deletes (namespace deletion leaks it).
**Fix:** strip only the controller ownerRef.
