### M-146 · `omitempty` on eleven numeric audit fields erases measured zeros — the audit record cannot distinguish "0" from "not computed" · **HIGH** · 🔴 LIVE · `RECORD`
**What:** `internal/audit/log.go` puts `,omitempty` on 22 of the 48 record fields, eleven of which are numeric or boolean: `threatRisk`, `perDollar`, `coveredWeight`, `coveredFacts`, `stageHazard`, `lambda`, `proactiveRate`, `preemptMargin`, `projectedNextStageSec`, `killSwitch`, `aiClassified`. For every one of these, zero is a *measured value*, not an absence — so the encoder deletes exactly the observations that matter and leaves an absent key that reads as "never computed".
**Live evidence (2026-08-30, corpus of 15,239 unique `phorvex.decision` records after deduplicating 18,872 raw SSE/audit capture lines, 2026-08-25T15:45→19:47Z, 6 tenants):**
- `lambda` appears in **0 of 15,239** unique records. Not because it is unset, but because it is exactly `0.00` on every one — the value survives only as text inside the human-readable `reason` string (`λ=0.00`). The single most load-bearing number in the economic claim is the one the structured record is guaranteed to omit.
- Erasure rates over the 15,239-record deduplicated corpus, with the median a naive reader gets versus the correct one:

| field | present | erased zeros | naive p50 (present cells only) | correct p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `preemptMargin` | 6,126 | **9,113 (59.8%)** | 0.3992 | **0** |
| `perDollar` | 920 | 14,319 (94.0%) | 27.0228 | **0** |
| `proactiveRate` | 920 | 14,319 (94.0%) | 27.0228 | **0** |
| `coveredWeight` | 920 | 14,319 (94.0%) | 3.821 | **0** |
| `stageHazard` | 15,016 | 223 (1.5%) | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| `lambda` | 0 | **15,239 (100%)** | n/a | **0** |

`preemptMargin` is the sharpest case: a reader who treats absent as missing reports a median of 0.3992 for a field whose median is 0. `lambda` is the worst: it has no present cells at all, so the field looks unimplemented rather than measured-zero.
**Why it matters here specifically:** this register's own live figures, and any observed-range export built from the audit stream, are wrong by default unless the reader knows which keys carry `omitempty`. An absent cell is not an unmeasured cell.
**Fix:** drop `omitempty` from every numeric and boolean field on the decision record (keep it on strings and slices where empty genuinely means absent). Zero must be emitted as `0`.
