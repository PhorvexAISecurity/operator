### M-94 · The SecurityEvent trust boundary is a self-declared string · **HIGH** · 🔴 LIVE (demonstrated by this campaign's own probes) · `BELIEF`
**What:** Phorvex ingests the cluster-wide SecurityEvent stream and trusts any event whose `source` isn't its own — anyone with securityevents-create can inject belief evidence (steer actuation) or, with delete (granted to the brain SA under console), withdraw it. Separately, `normalizeTarget("/name")` silently drops cluster-scoped-ref events.
**Live evidence (2026-08-21, incidental but decisive):** every live test in the second and third passes ran on forged events — `source: K3Detector`, a self-declared string from an identity with no prior trust relationship — and the brain trusted and acted on all of them: 24 selections on `pxv2t`, a real kv-reset actuation loop on the production vllm pod (V10), and the full Phoenix delete path in V2/V3. The trust boundary was not theorized; it was *used*, ~25 times, to steer the brain.
**Fix:** an allowlist of trusted detector identities (SA/namespace), enforced at ingest.
*Sources: A-176, I-25; live-demonstrated by the V1–V11 probe events.*
**Eleventh-pass status (`v0.4.74` / `515c56a`, 2026-09-03):** **Surface widened by `a79c555` (PR #92, v0.4.64+, in window).** Four new SecurityEvent types `phorvex.attack.{kv,guardrail,inflight,adapter}` (`internal/model/types.go:251-256`) are (i) promoted to `StagePersist` in `killchain/hazard.go:201-205` and (ii) used by recon to multiply one serving fact's weight ×3 (`recon/ucw.go:176-184`, `vectorDominance = 3.0` at `:276`) so the surgical actuator outranks endpoint-rotate. Both effects trigger on the event **type string alone** (`hasVectorEvent`, `:280-288`) — through exactly the self-declared trust boundary this entry proves forgeable. See **M-153**.
