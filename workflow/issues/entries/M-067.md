### M-67 · Warm-swap wedges on drain 404/timeout AFTER the leadership flip · **MAJOR** · 🔴 LIVE (T46) · `ACTUATION`
**What:** Cutover order: flip shadow to leader → relabel old leader retiring → drain → delete → rewarm. Stock vLLM has no `/v1/drain` (404) and a slow drain misclassifies as hard error — both abort AFTER the flip with no rollback: two leaders, a retiring pod still holding the GPU, no rewarm. The crash-resume reconciler then reaps the retiring pod — capacity silently drops by one, and the pool never replenishes. Observed live in the T46 warm-swap test.
**Fix:** treat 404/501 as "no drain endpoint" (continue); make drain best-effort or roll the flip back on failure.
*Sources: A-92, A-135.*
