### M-98 · C1 — the mathematical spine: value and cost are incommensurable, and λ can't fix it · **HIGH** · 🟠 STATIC · `ECONOMICS`
**What:** The objective adds an unbounded, policy-scaled value term to a normalized cost term; λ (the shadow price) is clamped at `max=10` with a fixed `0.05` step — at the ceiling the per-$ gate can't fire, and reaching the ceiling takes ~188 binding ticks. The dual subgradient adds a dimensionless move-count ratio to a value/cost-dimensioned price.
**Fix:** normalize the coverage side to a bounded scale OR make λ's range/step track realized value/cost; charge λ consistently (per-move OR per-cost).
*Sources: C1, C6, I-14.*
