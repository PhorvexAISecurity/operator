### M-11 · `Status.Actuated` is a write-once latch; `MovesThisWindow` counts selected, not enacted · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `CONTROL`
**What:** Once any actuation emits, `Actuated=true` forever. The moves counter increments on selection. A deployment that has enacted nothing since install still shows `Actuated=true`.
**Fix:** make `Actuated` a rolling-window condition; count enacted (Emitted) moves only.
*Sources: A-215.*
