### M-4 · The brain's own SA is over-privileged when the console is enabled · **HIGH** · 🔴 LIVE · `SURFACE`
**What:** With `console.enabled`, the chart grants the operator SA cluster-wide `pods/exec create`, `pods/log get`, and full `securityevents` (update/patch/delete) — on the same token the actuator path uses. The brain can rewrite its own policy CRs (`phorvexpolicies update/patch`), exec into any pod, and delete event evidence.
**Fix:** split the console into its own SA with its own Role; drop policy-write, pods/exec, and event-delete from the brain's identity.
*Sources: A-168, R3.*
