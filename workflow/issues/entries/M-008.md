### M-8 · Node-exec RBAC absent + misleading denial · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `CONTRACT`
**What:** The MIG node-exec path needs `pods/exec create` and a cluster-wide pod list; neither is granted anywhere. The denial is swallowed and reported as "no MIG node-agent pod Running on node" — a topology message for an authorization cause.
**Fix:** a third, separately-bound Role for `pods/exec` in the agent namespace only; propagate the real error.
*Sources: A-134.*
