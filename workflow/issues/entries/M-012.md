### M-12 · No actuation-failure metric · **HIGH** · 🔴 LIVE (third pass) · `ACTUATION->EFFECT`
**What:** Six distinct paths can enact nothing (EnsurePolicy error, dispatch error, Emitted:false, RBAC 403, re-gate veto, reconciler failure); none increments any counter. `phorvex_actuations_total` only goes up. No alert can fire on a flat success counter because there is no failure counter to compare.
**Live evidence (V10, 2026-08-21):** the deployed build's full metric surface was enumerated during a forced, repeated actuation failure (the V10 dead-endpoint kv-reset loop). `phorvex_actuations_total{action="kv-reset",tenant="vllm"}` stayed **flat at 122** throughout — the failures didn't increment the success counter (good) and didn't increment anything else either (the defect). The only failure-flavored series on the entire endpoint belong to the shadow calibrator (`phorvex_calib_refresh_total{result="failure"}`) and stock controller-runtime reconcile errors (0). An operator watching metrics during a total actuation outage sees *nothing*.
**Fix:** `phorvex_actuation_failures_total{tenant,action,reason}` + a circuit-open gauge.
*Sources: A-10; V10 live 2026-08-21.*
