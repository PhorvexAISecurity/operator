### M-174 · Observed decision cadence exceeds configured policy interval · **MEDIUM** · NEW · `CONTROL`

**What:** The published policy TickInterval is described as decision cadence, but a fixed global10s scheduler quantizes configured5s intervals to roughly10s and equal10s intervals can skip to roughly20s. Actual source replay and live per-policy counters corroborate the scheduler mechanism independently of SSE latency.

**Evidence:** intake/RPT-20260910-200300-policy-cadence/VERIFICATION.md

**Required correction:** Define and enforce the supported policy scheduling contract: either honor per-policy cadence with bounded scheduling and fairness, or explicitly constrain/validate supported intervals and document quantization. Cover below/equal/above-base intervals and positive controls without changing scoring or claiming strict real-time guarantees.
