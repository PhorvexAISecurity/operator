### M-156 · RLS prices `Unknown`/`Draining` phases at the idle floor — the learned path's cheapest rotation is the least-known pod · **HIGH once active** · 🔴 LIVE (dormant while λ pinned) · `ECONOMICS` · MATH-GATE
**What:** Two encodings of the same `model.WorkloadPhase` vocabulary disagree on the two least-known phases. The heuristic path (`cost/switching.go` `flowCost`) prices `Unknown` at **0.5** (neutral) and `Draining` at **0.2**. The learned path (`cost/rls.go`) assigns neither a feature bit, so both collapse to the bias-only prior **0.15** — the idle *floor*, the cheapest rotation the RLS path can quote. Today this is dormant: λ is pinned and the learner ships frozen, so `Predict` at the prior is what selection would see. **Activation condition:** the day λ un-pins / RLS unfreezes in production (M-139's "the day λ un-pins, cost correctness matters immediately" — sibling entries, same trigger), every Unknown-phase pod — precisely the pods whose telemetry failed or whose phase could not be determined — reads as the *cheapest* pod to rotate on the learned path, while the heuristic path reads it as neutral. That is the T1-D "why it matters" regression: a large fraction of decisions (Unknown+Draining were 28.5% of the validation corpus) would use the implicit, cheapest category.
**Why not fixed in T1-D (2026-09-05):** T1-D's Math gate is **NO** and its trap forbids assigning a point cost to `Unknown` without a Math ruling. T1-D does what is engineering-legal: makes the divergence *explicit and single-sourced* — both encodings derive from one `phaseFlowEncoding` table (`cost/phase_encoding.go`, branch `t1/economics-exhaustive-phase-vocabulary`), so the day Math rules, exactly one number changes in exactly one place, and the table row carries a pointer here.
**Fix (Math ruling required):** decide the principled price of *not knowing* a pod's phase on the learned path — conservative-above-neutral (matching or exceeding switching's 0.5), a dedicated feature bit with its own prior, or exclusion of Unknown-phase candidates from learned-path pricing. Whatever the ruling, it must also pick `Draining`'s learned encoding (0.15 vs 0.2 gap, same mechanism, smaller stakes).
