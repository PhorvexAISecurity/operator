### M-142 · `Telemetry.Present` is universally true (cAdvisor), so `PhaseOf`'s conservative Warm fallback is dead past the first scrape interval — the fleet is priced Idle under arbitrary load · **HIGH** · 🟡 TYPE-LEVEL HALF FIXED (live-verified 2026-09-05, deployed `0.4.84` @ `4522076`); cAdvisor-delta phase boundary + constants remain → T1-M142 · `SENSING`
**What:** `ingest/prometheus.go:135` sets `Telemetry.Present = true` if **any** signal returns for a pod. cAdvisor metrics (`container_network_*`) are universal, so every pod has `Present=true` within one scrape interval of birth. `PhaseOf` (`ingest/kube.go:610-628`) routes `Present=true` pods with `ReqRate≤0.001 && GPUUtil≤0.01` to **Idle** — and only those two signals consult into the boundary. The author's conservative fallback (*"Ready, no telemetry: assume warm — rotating costs the warm-load"*) is therefore unreachable for the entire pod lifetime except the first ~30–60s before cAdvisor's first sample (observed exactly once, on a freshly created pod's first record: `phase:"Warm"` at age 20s, then `Idle` forever). Consequence: any pod not self-reporting `http_requests_total` (nginx, and in production: litellm, crm, voice, mcp, chroma) is classified **Idle under arbitrary load**, and rotate is priced at the idle rate fleet-wide.
**Live evidence (Round 1, 2026-08-25, `phorvex:0.4.50`):** production litellm pod reads `phase:"Idle"` in the decision audit stream **while under active synthetic traffic** (the cluster-wide loadgen drives litellm at ~3 sessions/min, `POST /v1/chat/completions` 200 OKs throughout — this is not the hypothetical uninstrumented case, it is the loaded case, already captured). Controlled confirmation: Round 2a (uninstrumented nginx under load) with 2b (instrumented) as its positive control.
**Fix:** make `Present` mean "an app-level signal is present" (ReqRate/GPUUtil/queue-depth), not "cAdvisor is alive"; or consult ConnCount/bytes deltas into the phase boundary so cAdvisor-visible load moves phase.
**T1-C cross-reference (2026-09-04, cycle 4):** fixed by the typed per-signal `Signal{Value,State,Age,Src}` retype (`fixes/T1-C/03-decision.md`) — `PhaseOf` keys on `AppLevelPresent`, never any-signal presence. Manifestation list += one found in cycle-4 blind derivation: `physics/littleslaw.go`'s fan-out term divided real cAdvisor ConnCount by an absent-zero ReqRate, fabricating a "lateral probing" residual on every uninstrumented pod into LIVE selection; resolved by per-term `Usable()` gates (no physics-math change).
**Half-closure (2026-09-05, joint L3 at `0.4.84` @ `4522076`):** the type-level half is live-verified — fleet-wide Idle→Warm collapse (`warm-no-app-signal`) on exactly the uninstrumented pods this entry names (litellm, crm, voice, mcp, chroma); the one instrumented idle pod (vllm, reqRate present=0) stays Idle (control 2b); phantom fan-out = 0 on every absent-reqRate record; a rotate's `expectedCost` now carries the Warm phase price (0.70, observed live in actuation). The cAdvisor-delta phase boundary (ConnCount/bytes deltas moving phase) and its constants are deliberately NOT in T1-C — they remain here, owned by the future T1-M142 ticket.
