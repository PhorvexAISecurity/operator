### M-27 · Kill-chain belief never ages events · **HIGH** · 🟠 STATIC · `CLOCK->BELIEF`
**What:** Every `SecurityEvent` CR is ingested every tick with no TTL or age cutoff; the evidence sum and stage escalation never read event age (a regression test asserts byte-identical output for 0s vs 30min-old events). The only bound is an unenforced assumption that detectors delete resolved CRs. Long-lived clusters drift toward a permanent all-attacked steady state.
**Fix:** age the evidence over a half-life, or enforce CR TTL.
*Sources: N3.*
