### M-48 · Quarantine NetworkPolicy: stale-UID selector after pod-name recycle · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** The NP is named by pod NAME but selects by pod UID; Create tolerates AlreadyExists without reconciling the spec; GC keys on name. A StatefulSet respawn leaves an NP selecting a dead UID — zero containment, reported as success.
**Fix:** name the NP by UID, or get-then-update the selector; ownerRef the NP to the pod.
*Sources: A-56.*
