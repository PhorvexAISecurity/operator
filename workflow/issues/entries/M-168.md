### M-168 · Console availability experiment claims attack results despite failed stimulus and unsafe restart recovery · **HIGH** · NEW · `VERIFICATION`

**What:** Three-arm runner invokes absent curl on hardcoded localhost8095 while shipped console listens8090, ignores the fire result, and renders hardcoded attacker-stopped verdicts. It mutates policy mode without a durable operation-owned restore contract, so restart cannot establish safe restoration and old state can overwrite later edits. Source and captured invocation verified, not a live destructive experiment.

**Evidence:** intake/RPT-20260910-184000-console-experiment-validity/VERIFICATION.md; assessments/20260910-readiness/fresh-console/LEAD-REVIEW-R1.md

**Required correction:** Execute a supported authenticated stimulus with explicit outcome checks; derive security claims from causally bound observations; journal experiment-owned mutations with UID/RV/lease identity and bounded fail-closed recovery without overwriting later user changes. Incomplete stimulus, cleanup or observation cannot be a completed comparison.
