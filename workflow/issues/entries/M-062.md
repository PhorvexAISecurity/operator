### M-62 · `endpoint-rotate` is two non-atomic writes with a blackhole window · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** The pod is re-keyed FIRST (instantly ejecting it from the Service), then the Service selector is restitched. Between the writes the Service has zero endpoints; a Service-write failure (or crash) strands it permanently — no reconciler repairs it. On single-replica serving (the demo topology) this is a real traffic gap, contradicting the "zero downtime" demo narration.
**Fix:** restitch to a both-keys selector first, then re-key, then tighten; retry on conflict; a `ResumeEndpointRotations` reconciler.
*Sources: A-87, A-145.*
