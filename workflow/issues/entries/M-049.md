### M-49 · Quarantine idempotency shortcut never rechecks the NetworkPolicy · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** If the pod label says quarantined, the handler returns success without Getting the NP. Anyone (GitOps prune, cleanup, attacker with NP delete) can remove the NP; every subsequent quarantine reports containment while the pod has full network access. (Native-actuator path only.)
**Fix:** hoist the NP-ensure above the label shortcut; re-assert the detach.
*Sources: A-28, A-61.*
