### M-17 · Audit integrity: written after actuation, write errors discarded, zero-backup rotation erases the log · **MINOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `RECORD`
**What:** Ordering is deliberate (audit reflects what fired) but a crash between actuation and write loses the record; JSONL write errors are swallowed; `--audit-max-backups=0` deletes the rolled live file.
**Fix:** fsync + write-error metric; guard the zero-backup path.
*Sources: A-18, A-153, A-220.*
