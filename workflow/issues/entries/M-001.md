### M-1 · The shipped RBAC cannot actuate; the live demos ran on a hand-applied ClusterRole · **MAJOR** · 🔴 LIVE · `CONTRACT`
**What:** The Helm chart's ClusterRole is read-only for pods/networkpolicies ("NEVER writes pods" in its own header). The committed least-privilege write Role (`config/rbac/phorvex-actuator-role.yaml`) binds a dedicated `phorvex-actuator` ServiceAccount that **the code never uses** — the actuator writes through the manager's own client — and that **exists in no namespace** on the cluster (verified). Live `auth can-i` as the brain SA: `delete pods` cluster-wide = **no**.
**Why the demos worked anyway:** an ad-hoc ClusterRole `phorvex-actuation` (pods get/list/watch/delete/update/patch, networkpolicies full, services get/update/patch) was hand-applied and bound to the **manager SA** in `vllm` and `litellm` only — exactly the shared-token pattern the committed Role's header disclaims. Anyone redeploying from the chart gets a brain whose hands are denied; the failure is silent (no failure metric, status shows "selected").
**Also:** no `pods/create` grant exists anywhere → the warm-swap rewarm path can never succeed (preempts M-72/M-73). The committed Role also lacks `services` verbs (endpoint-rotate needs them) and `pods/exec` (node-exec needs it).
**Fix:** template the actuator Role/RoleBinding from the chart per managed namespace, bound to the SA the operator actually runs as (or real impersonation); refuse to start with `actuator: native|hybrid` when writes are denied (fail-fast at boot, not per-tick 403s).
*Sources: A-1, A-58, A-167, A-171.*
**Live re-verification (K3, 2026-09-03, deployed `0.4.74`):** all of it still true, with sharper drift detail. The hand-applied ClusterRole `phorvex-actuation` persists (exists nowhere in the repo), bound via RoleBinding in `vllm` (66d) and `litellm` (71d) only. Its current rules vs the committed Role A: **overgrants** — pods `+patch +watch`, services `+patch`, networkpolicies full verb set, and `pods/ephemeralcontainers get/create/update/patch` (the code-exec verbs the committed Role B declares OFF BY DEFAULT and unbound — live, they are bound wherever this ClusterRole is bound); **undergrants** — no `replicasets:get`, which the committed Role A marks load-bearing for quarantine's RS-detach. The chart's namespaced actuator Roles are installed nowhere. Meanwhile `voice` rotations actuate with **no native forensics** in their audit records (no `handler`/`objectsTouched`/`mutationKind`) — the Phoenix/AMTD path, which the manager-role permits cluster-wide — so tenants without the hand-applied binding rotate via Phoenix while `vllm`/`litellm` use the native path. Two actuation planes, neither matching the committed design.
