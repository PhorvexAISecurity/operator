### M-41 · `kv-reset` flushes one pod of a Service · **HIGH** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** The flush POSTs to the target pod's IP only. On multi-replica serving, siblings keep the poisoned cache while the session fact is booked covered for the asset.
**Fix:** fan out over the Service's ready endpoints, or scope the fact per-pod.
*Sources: A-95.*
