### M-77 · `postJSON`: classifier asymmetry + single-read truncation · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** The sibling client substring-matches `"timeout"` (lowercase) — Go's timeout string is `Client.Timeout exceeded` (capital T) — so timeouts fall to the fail-hard branch, the opposite of `do()`. It also reads the body with ONE `Read` into a 16KiB buffer (short reads legal), so the drain abort-count is fabricated on chunked responses. The two clients disagree about the same network event, and warm-swap takes the worst of both (M-67).
**Fix:** one shared classifier; `io.ReadAll`/`io.LimitReader`.
*Sources: A-36, A-86, A-102, A-112, A-122, A-189.*
