### M-57 · `debugger` injects a corpse · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** busybox with nil Command, Stdin/TTY false → the container reads EOF and exits immediately; no TargetContainerName → no shared PID namespace; nothing ever execs into it. The pod pays an irreversible spec mutation for a container no one can use. Name-keyed idempotency means a retry after fixing anything returns "already present."
**Fix:** `sleep`-style persistent command, Stdin/TTY true, TargetContainerName set, documented attach flow.
*Sources: A-73.*
