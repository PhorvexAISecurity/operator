### M-38 · `quarantine`'s detach half only works for ReplicaSet-owned pods · **MAJOR** · 🟠 STATIC · `ACTUATION`
**What:** The selector-break resolves only ReplicaSet owners; StatefulSet/Job/DaemonSet/bare pods keep their labels, stay in every Service's endpoints, and get re-adopted by their controller — while the network cover (networkIdentity + all lateralEdges) is booked. The feasibility laws steer stateful pods into exactly this path (rotate is vetoed on them).
**Fix:** resolve any controller owner's selector (via unstructured Get), or fall back to breaking Service-matching keys.
*Sources: A-44, A-55.*
