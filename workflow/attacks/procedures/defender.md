# M6/R3 — adversarial signals and defender-induced disruption

Use only an approved fixture identity and target. Establish healthy traffic and
whether the fixture principal can submit the synthetic signal. Pre-register which
signals should be trusted, the response budget, and a genuine-alert control.

Submit a bounded forged/replayed signal sequence. Observe decision attribution,
trust rejection, rate/cooldown behavior, workload churn and request impact. Then
prove a legitimate alert can still obtain the approved response. Do not treat
"no action" as success if all detection is broken.

PASS means the stated trust/rate contract and genuine-alert control both hold;
otherwise FAIL with defense_outcome unmitigated or bounded. Synthetic signal
injection is the attack here, not natural evidence of a separate compromise.
Abort at disruption ceilings; remove only owned events and verify no residual
churn. Historical references: T13/T44.
