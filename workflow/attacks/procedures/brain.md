# Brain and recovery probes

Use the named probe's prerequisites and a pinned isolated fixture. Declare
multiple genuinely feasible alternatives; hold policy fixed across treatment and
control. Observe candidate exclusions, scores, dispatch and independent effects.
Do not use self-reported cost as a measurement of actual disruption.

- P-CHOICE: vary attack evidence and independent measured disruption; compare
  choices against pre-registered acceptable sets, including safe no-action.
- P-RBAC: test Phoenix/native/hybrid routing with the correct acting principal;
  paired denied/allowed cases must disagree for the right permission.
- P-FAILURE: inject a bounded failure or acknowledgement without effect; verify
  accurate outcome, bounded retry, recovery and no false confirmation.
- P-COOLDOWN: test fresh pods, repeated signals and clock boundaries; distinguish
  last action from pod birth and observe the declared response/cooldown windows.
- P-TELEMETRY: vary missing/stale/contradictory data against a measured-zero control;
  verify honest uncertainty and the declared safe action policy.

All probes need discriminating controls, request-impact measurements and cleanup.
Capability absence is BLOCKED. Findings enter existing intake; a failed hypothesis
is not repaired by changing thresholds after the run. These test brain contracts,
not attack prevalence. No broad shared-cluster fault injection is implied.
