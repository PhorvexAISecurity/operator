# Historical application-boundary case

This case is a threat-design reference, not an automatically approved exploit.
Read only its entry in the historical attack report and ledger. Before execution,
write a concrete `run_procedure` in the owning fix/campaign evidence directory:
applicable version/configuration, authorized target/identity, exact bounded steps,
independent attack-success oracle, control, time/cost ceilings and cleanup.
A patched/absent surface is NOT_APPLICABLE with evidence, not successful defense.

Distinguish an outside-in compromise from a supplied foothold or direct fixture
write. Preserve Phorvex enrollment, detector availability and feasible-action
context. Prove data/access outcomes independently; serving a poisoned tool string
alone does not prove the downstream agent acted on it. The expected boundary may
be unmitigated; passing that test documents the limitation, not a defense win.

Abort outside approved scope. Cleanup removes/revokes only owned artifacts.
All findings enter existing intake. Validate every CVE-to-mechanism mapping anew;
realism scores are historical judgments and are never an execution permission.
