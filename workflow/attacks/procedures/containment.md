# GP10/Q1/GP6/GP8 — containment

Approve target state/availability constraints first; do not lower a shared
production availability floor to manufacture selection. Establish real permitted
lateral/egress traffic and data-integrity baseline in an isolated fixture.

Trigger the declared signal, preserve the supported candidate set, and trace the
backend/principal used. After quarantine, test both existing connections and new
connections from the target to the same destinations, including Service DNS.
Read-back of a NetworkPolicy alone is insufficient: enforcement must block the
traffic. Check ownership/re-adoption, replicas, stored data and recovery.

PASS requires the specified edges blocked while the agreed state/availability
contract holds. With no feasible safe action, record a separate safe-decline
observation; do not claim containment. Abort on data-risk or uncontrolled replica
creation. Remove owned isolation and verify traffic/data recover as planned.
Historical references: Q1/Q2/Q3, T6/T9/T34/T35/T41.
