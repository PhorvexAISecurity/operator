**Conclusion:** DCP profiling metrics are NOT enabled (default collector set; no
`DCGM_EXPORTER_COLLECTORS`, no `-f <csv>`). Enable-able only by patching the DaemonSet (the image ships
`/etc/dcgm-exporter/dcp-metrics-included.csv`); whether the T4 + driver 580.159.03 then actually emit
`PROF_SM_ACTIVE` is an empirical question that requires that patch experiment — a telemetry-stack change
that stays human-approved per FIX_LOOP §1. PCIe PROF counters exist but are throughput, not SM activity.
