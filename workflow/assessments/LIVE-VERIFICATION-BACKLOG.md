## How to use this list

- Keep the IDs stable. Add the exact source/digests, fixture identity, time window, positive and negative controls, outcome, cleanup and evidence link when a row is exercised. Close only the issue-specific controls actually demonstrated; one successful shared fixture does not automatically close every related issue.
- The lead prepares and independently verifies tests. DevOps owns deployment, grants, infrastructure and any shared restart. The owner approves the disposable workload/test scope where needed. No new shared mutation or credential replay is authorized by this file.
- Reuse one suitable fixture and one evidence collection for compatible controls to save time/cost. Keep individual expected outcomes and verdicts. Missing hardware or an absent positive control remains pending.
- Review this backlog before each tier's live acceptance, milestone decision and campaign completion. Starting a later source tier does not erase earlier verification debt. Production recommendation remains HOLD until applicable release criteria are satisfied.
