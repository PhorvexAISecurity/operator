# System assessment and recommendations

## Scope and evidence

State source/deployment pins and completed, partial and untested phases. Link coverage,
raw evidence manifest, analysis and independent review. Distinguish known-correct behavior
from defects, research directions and unknowns. Do not claim full-pipeline validation from
static analysis or selection logs alone.

## Findings and register reconciliation

Link reviewed candidates and root-cause comparisons, existing issue updates and new intake
records. Explain corrections to historical conclusions and unresolved cases. Counts must be
derived from those records, not copied from an old report banner.

## Module recommendations

For each: what it is; current implementation and rationale; correctness; static and live
evidence; proposed change and alternatives; confidence; dependencies; acceptance experiment.
For constants: units/source/calibration status, observed range and conditions, sample size,
sensitivity and uncertainty. Unsupported numerical recommendations remain INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE.

## System recommendations

Explain cross-component contracts, structural alternatives, expected benefit, tradeoffs,
review evidence and dependencies. Separate instrumentation enablers from product fixes.

## Follow-up and final review

Prioritize unresolved experiments and fixes. Link campaign cleanup/verdicts. Record reviewer,
challenges, corrections, residual gaps and final report disposition. Report completion does
not close issues or waive their fix-level live verification.
