# Campaign

## Question and scope

State the cross-fix or system question, release/fix set, environment,
population, duration and cost ceiling.

## Scientific design

Reference `hypotheses.yaml`. Separate baseline, intervention, positive control,
sample unit, falsifier and stopping rule.

## Safety boundary

List permissions, prohibited actions, phase approvals, mutation ledger and
cleanup owner.

## Rounds

Each round keeps its specification, observations, analysis, independent
review, cleanup and evidence manifest separate.

## Findings

Create intake records; do not edit Master Issues directly.
